NetworkConditionsFloret

public class NetworkConditionsFloret : InterceptingFloret
extension NetworkConditionsFloret: DisplayingFloret

The NetworkConditionsFloret simulates slow and unreliable networks: it delays requests and responses and lets a share of them fail, with a NetworkCondition per host.

let conditions = NetworkConditionsFloret()
let cauli = Cauli([InspectorFloret(), conditions])
conditions.defaultCondition = .threeG
conditions.setCondition(.offline, for: "api.example.com")

The conditions and every host the app connected to are kept across app starts. Responses reach the app at once, so bandwidth limits are simulated as a delay. Only requests Cauli intercepts are affected.
